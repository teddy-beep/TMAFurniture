Our Story

A legacy of artisanal metalwork passed down through generations, crafting beauty from raw metal since 1998.

The Beginning

Tilahun Metal Art Furniture was born from a passion for transforming raw metal into functional art. Founded in 1998 by master craftsman Tilahun Abebe, our workshop began as a small space in the heart of Addis Ababa's industrial district.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into a renowned atelier, but our commitment to traditional hand-forging techniques remains unchanged. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the same dedication to quality and artistry that inspired our founder over two decades ago.

Today, we continue to push the boundaries of metal furniture design, blending time-honored craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics to create pieces that are both beautiful and built to last generations.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped our company and defined our commitment to excellence.

1998 - The Beginning

Tilahun Abebe establishes a small metalworking workshop in Addis Ababa, focusing on custom ironwork and repairs.

2003 - First Major Commission

Completed our first large-scale dining set for a local hotel, establishing our reputation for quality craftsmanship.

2008 - Workshop Expansion

Expanded to a larger facility and hired our first team of apprentices, beginning the tradition of passing down skills.

2013 - International Recognition

Featured in regional design magazines and began receiving commissions from international clients.

2018 - 20th Anniversary

Celebrated two decades of craftsmanship with a landmark exhibition showcasing our finest works.

2024 - Present Day

Now a leading name in luxury metal furniture, with a team of skilled artisans and clients worldwide.
